## Page 2: Understanding the Experience

**Meta title:** Understanding Dental Anxiety \| The Anxious Patient Experience™

**Meta description:** Dental anxiety often starts in the body, not the mind. Learn why it happens and how we work with your nervous system instead of against it.

### Hero

**Eyebrow:** Understanding the experience

**Headline:** What if your dental care started with understanding your anxiety first?

**Subheading:** Not built around it.

### Why your body reacts

**Eyebrow:** The nervous system

**Heading:** Dental anxiety often starts in the body.

**Body:** Your nervous system is always checking whether you are safe. At the dentist, many things can tell it you are not:

-   Unfamiliar environments

-   Past experiences

-   Claustrophobia

-   Needles

-   Sounds and sensations

-   Embarrassment

-   Unpredictability

### How it can show up

**Heading:** What it can feel like

**Body:** When your body feels unsafe, you might notice:

-   A racing heart

-   Muscle tension

-   Changes in your breathing

-   Shaking

-   Nausea

-   Tears

-   Freezing

-   The urge to leave or cancel

**Closing line:** This is not always a conscious choice. It is your body trying to protect you.

### Our approach

**Eyebrow:** Our approach

**Heading:** We don\'t ask you to ignore that response. We work with it.

**Body:** Being told to \"just relax\" rarely helps. Instead, we help you understand what is happening, give you practical tools to regulate that response and create an environment where accessing care feels possible.

### What we do differently

**Eyebrow:** What we do differently

**Heading:** Care designed around how you feel.

  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Card                                              Description
  ------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Understanding you before you arrive               We learn about your history, concerns and preferences before your first visit.

  Predictability                                    You know what will happen, when it will happen and how long it will take.

  Control                                           You set the pace and can ask to pause at any time.

  Communication tailored to you                     We explain as much or as little as you want, in the way that suits you.

  Nervous-system regulation                         Practical techniques to help your body settle before and during care.

  Sensory support                                   Options to help with sound, light and touch, including headphones and choosing what you watch or listen to.

  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

### About sedation

**Heading:** Sedation is one option, not the whole plan.

**Body:** Where clinically appropriate, sedation may include nitrous oxide or IV sedation. Our broader aim is to help you build regulation skills and confidence, so you are not entirely reliant on sedation to access care.

### An honest note

**Heading:** Is this approach enough on its own?

**Body:** The Anxious Patient Experience™ may not be sufficient as a standalone approach for every patient. More severe dental phobia or complex cases may require pharmacological support or additional care. If that applies to you, we will talk it through with you and recommend the right next step.

## Page 5: FAQs

**Meta title:** FAQs \| The Anxious Patient Experience™

**Meta description:** Answers to common questions about The Anxious Patient Experience™, including sedation, costs and what happens at your first appointment.

### Hero

**Eyebrow:** Questions

**Headline:** Everything you might want to know before you begin.

### Questions and answers

**Dev note:** Accordion layout.

**Am I suitable for The Anxious Patient Experience™?**

If your body tenses, your heart races or you avoid the dentist because of how it makes you feel, this was created for you. It may not be sufficient on its own for everyone. More severe dental phobia or complex cases may need additional support, which we will discuss with you.

**What if I haven\'t been to the dentist for years?**

You are not alone, and there is no judgement here. We start from where you are today.

**What if I become overwhelmed or cry?**

That\'s okay. It\'s a natural response, not something to apologise for. We pause, give you time and only continue when you are ready.

**Do I have to have treatment at my first appointment?**

No. Your first appointment is about understanding you and what you need. Any treatment is discussed and planned with you, at your pace.

**Can I bring someone with me?**

Yes. You\'re welcome to bring someone you trust.

**Can I use headphones or choose what I watch or listen to?**

Yes. Bring your own headphones and choose what you watch or listen to during your visit.

**Is sedation available?**

Where clinically appropriate, additional support such as nitrous oxide or IV sedation may be available. Our broader aim is to help you build regulation skills and confidence, so you are not entirely reliant on sedation.

**What happens after my first appointment?**

We talk through what we learned together and agree on the next steps. You decide what happens next and when.

**How much does it cost?**

The New Patient Appointment is 70 minutes and costs \$350. A \$100 deposit is required to secure your booking.

**What is the cancellation and deposit policy?**

With 48 hours\' notice or more, your deposit can be transferred or refunded. With less than 48 hours\' notice or non-attendance, your deposit may be retained. If anxiety is making it hard to attend, please contact us rather than simply not attending.
